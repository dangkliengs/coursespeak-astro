The CourseSpeak blog is a daily-updated list of Udemy coupon articles. Each article shows the top free Udemy courses and verified discount codes for that specific date, so you can find 100% off coupons and enroll in premium courses for free.

How often are new Udemy coupon articles published?

We publish a new article every day. Each post contains that day's verified Udemy coupons and free courses, grouped by category. You can browse by date from this blog index.

Are the Udemy coupons in these articles really free?

Yes. Every coupon we list gives you 100% off the course price — you pay nothing. We verify each code before publishing. No credit card or subscription is required to enroll via these links.

How do I use the daily coupon articles?

Pick a date from the blog grid above. Each article opens a page with that day's Udemy coupons, sorted by category. Click a course to go to its deal page, then use the coupon link to enroll on Udemy for free.

Besides the blog, you can browse all current deals on our Deals page, filter by category, or use search. The blog is best for seeing what was added on a specific day.
